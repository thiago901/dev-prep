## Scope

The DevPrep application shell and every study surface inside it: Home (the lab
console), Learn (paths), Content detail and its activity kinds, Today's Practice
and its runner, Speaking Practice, Library, Flashcards, English Practice, Mock
Interview, Progress, Profile, Admin builder. Visitor mode: **Operate**.

## Audience and task

Mid-to-senior developers rehearsing interview answers out loud, in English,
usually alone and late. The task is one take at a time: read the prompt, speak,
hear yourself, then compare against a model answer. Constraints: microphone
permission is a normal failure state; some sessions are silent by necessity;
mobile is a first-class recording device; nothing may sit between the user and
their answer.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The two-channel language lab position: your attempt on channel one,
the model answer on channel two, and channel two is physically inert until
channel one has signal. What counts as an attempt is set by the work — reading a
briefing, answering a check, making a call, writing it out, or speaking it — and
the microphone is the tool for the last of those, not the toll booth in front of
all of them. The product teaches in rungs: Learn, Recognise, Decide, Apply,
Explain, Speak. It refuses the study dashboard this category always ships — no
stat tiles, no progress rings, no streak number, no greeting banner. Attempt
before answer is not a setting, it is the hardware.

**OWN-WORLD.** A warm acoustically-dead booth ground (near-black brown, never
blue-black), tape-deck chassis panels one step lighter, bone silkscreen legends,
brass VU illumination as the single warm accent, a record red that exists only
while armed, and a sage monitor green for level-OK. Channel two sits cool
blue-grey and desaturated while locked. Components are channel strips, transport
rows, level meters, drill cards, take sheets and lamp legends. Archivo Expanded
for tracked faceplate legends, Archivo for text, JetBrains Mono for code,
counters and timecode. Color appears at edges, lamps and meters; the reading
field stays achromatic.

**STORY.** The visitor understands within one viewport what they should do next
and what will happen when they press it. They see one recommendation, not four
equal options, and they can tell a briefing from a decision from a spoken answer
before committing to any of them. They believe the model answer is being withheld
on purpose. By the end of a path they are speaking an answer they could not have
given at the start.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** A thin faceplate header carries the wordmark left and the
channel controls right (language as a real two-position switch). Beneath it, one
recommended section dominates: the largest heading, the progress rail of whatever
it continues, the mix of activity kinds it holds, and the screen's only lit key —
brass, because starting is not recording. The alternatives follow at body scale
with Neutral keys. To the right a monitor bank of skill channel strips with
levels and state lamps, and what is due underneath. No card grid, no tiles.

**FORM.** The Language Lab, candidate 1 of my ordered grounded list, chosen by
the user over the assigned roll through the structured fallback channel after the
decision page closed unanswered. Seed key 1736e25a, mode operate, scope
direction, telemetry kind `pick`. Code-led: no image generation exists in this
harness, so the ambition rides in this contract and the signature interaction
below.

**Signature interaction.** The channel-two unlock: finishing an attempt — a take,
a written answer, or the last statement of a check — releases the locked channel
with a mechanical engage, its strike-through clears, its fader goes live, and the
answer unrolls. Its sibling is the decision card: it drags under the thumb, the
side legend lights in the direction of travel, and letting go commits the call. Compare cross-fades your take against the
model's structure. Motion is transport-mechanical and short: controls depress,
lamps engage, nothing floats. Reduced motion makes lamps static and transitions
instant.

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance

## Memorable moment

The first time channel two is still struck through and silent while the record
lamp is live: the product visibly refuses to answer until you have. Its quieter
twin, four rungs earlier: a decision card that lets go under the thumb and comes
back with "sua decisão / decisão esperada" — and they do not match, for a reason
the panel then explains.
